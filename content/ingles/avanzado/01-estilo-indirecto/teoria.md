## En una frase

El estilo indirecto (**reported speech**) sirve para contar lo que otra persona dijo, preguntó o pidió: "The server is down" → She said that the server was down. En el trabajo lo usas todo el tiempo: en la daily, al resumir una llamada con el cliente o al pasarle un mensaje a tu líder.

## Cómo se forma

**Say** y **tell**: tell siempre lleva a quién se lo dijeron; say no.
- She **said that** she was busy. → Dijo que estaba ocupada.
- She **told me that** she was busy. → Me dijo que estaba ocupada.

El verbo "retrocede" un paso cuando cuentas lo que alguien dijo antes:

| Lo que dijo | Lo que cuentas |
|---|---|
| "I **work** here." | He said that he **worked** there. |
| "I**'m fixing** it." | She said that she **was fixing** it. |
| "I **fixed** it." / "I**'ve fixed** it." | He said that he **had fixed** it. |
| "I**'ll** call you." | She said that she **would** call me. |
| "I **can** help." | He said that he **could** help. |

Si lo que dijo sigue siendo verdad, puedes dejar el tiempo igual: Tom said that the demo is on Friday (y sigue siendo el viernes).

Cambian también las referencias: today → **that day**, tomorrow → **the next day**, yesterday → **the day before**, last week → **the previous week**, next week → **the following week**, now → **then**, here → **there**, this → **that**, y los pronombres ("I'll send **you**..." → She said that she would send **me**...).

Preguntas: orden de frase normal, sin do/does/did. Si es de sí o no, usa **if**.
- "Where is the file?" → He asked me where **the file was**.
- "Did you test it?" → She asked **if I had tested** it.

Órdenes y pedidos: **tell / ask + persona + to + verbo** (negativo: **not to**).
- "Wait a minute." → He told me **to wait** a minute.
- "Don't push to main." → She told us **not to push** to main.

Otros verbos de reporte: **explain** (that, how), **mention** (that), **admit**, **deny**, **claim**, **confirm**, **promise**.

**That** se puede omitir al hablar (She said she was busy). En las frases de esta unidad lo escribimos siempre, para que veas la estructura completa.

## Errores típicos

- She said me that she was busy ✗ → She **told me** that she was busy ✓ (say no lleva persona; si la pones: said **to** me).
- He explained me the bug ✗ → He explained the bug **to me** ✓
- She asked me where was the file ✗ → She asked me where **the file was** ✓
- He asked me if I did test it ✗ → He asked me if I **had tested** it ✓
- He told me that I wait ✗ → He told me **to wait** ✓ (calco de "me dijo que esperara").
- Contándolo una semana después: She said that she will come tomorrow ✗ → She said that she **would** come **the next day** ✓

## Pronunciación

- **said** se dice "séd", no "séid". Rima con red.
- **asked** termina en "skt": "áskt". Si dices solo "ás", suena a presente (ask).
- **told** "tóuld": la l final se oye suave, no es "tol".
- **whether** suena igual que weather: "uéder".
