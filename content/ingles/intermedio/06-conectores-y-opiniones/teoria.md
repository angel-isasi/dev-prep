## En una frase

Los conectores unen tus ideas y las opiniones las ordenan: In my opinion, React is a good choice **because** the team knows it. **However**, it's heavier than Vue. Con ellos puedes defender una decisión técnica en una entrevista o en una reunión sin sonar cortante.

## Cómo se forma

| Para... | Conector | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Dar la razón | because, because of, due to | We chose Vite because it's fast. |
| Dar el resultado | so, therefore, consequently | The tests failed, so we didn't deploy. |
| Contrastar | but, however, although, even though, despite | Although it works, it's hard to read. |
| Comparar lados | on the other hand | Vue is simple. On the other hand, React has more jobs. |
| Sumar | also, besides, in addition | Besides, the docs are excellent. |
| Dar ejemplos | for example, such as | Libraries such as React or Vue... |
| Cerrar | overall, to sum up | To sum up, I recommend the simpler option. |

Dónde van:
- **although / even though / because** + oración completa, en medio o al inicio: Although it's slow, it works.
- **however / therefore** empiezan una oración nueva y llevan coma: It works. However, it's slow.
- **despite / because of / due to** + sustantivo: despite the deadline, because of the rain.

Opiniones:
- Dar: I think..., In my opinion..., As far as I know..., Frankly...
- Estar de acuerdo: I agree. Absolutely. That makes sense. Exactly.
- No estar de acuerdo con cortesía: I see your point, but... / I'm not sure about that. / I disagree, because...

## Errores típicos

- I am agree ✗ → I agree ✓ (agree es un verbo, no un adjetivo).
- In my opinion, I think... ✗ → In my opinion... o I think... ✓ (es redundante).
- Although it works, but it's slow ✗ → Although it works, it's slow ✓ (no se usan los dos).
- Despite it was late ✗ → Although it was late ✓ / Despite the time ✓
- The tests failed, therefore we didn't deploy ✗ → The tests failed. Therefore, we didn't deploy ✓ (therefore empieza otra oración).
- According to me ✗ → In my opinion ✓

## Pronunciación

- **however** "jauéver": la fuerza va en "é".
- **although** "olzóu": la "gh" no suena, y la th es como una d suave con la lengua entre los dientes.
- **therefore** "zérfor": la fuerza al inicio.
- **opinion** "opínion", nunca "opinión" con fuerza al final.
