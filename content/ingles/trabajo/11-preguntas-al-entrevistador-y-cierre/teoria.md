# Preguntas al entrevistador y cierre

## En una frase

Casi todas las entrevistas terminan con "Do you have any questions for us?": nunca respondas "No". Ten dos o tres preguntas listas y una forma clara de hablar de disponibilidad, salario y modalidad.

## Cómo se forma

**Tus preguntas** (elige dos o tres):

| Tema | Pregunta |
|---|---|
| El equipo | **How big is the team**, and who would I work with most? |
| El stack | **What does your tech stack look like?** |
| Onboarding | **What does onboarding look like** for a junior developer? |
| Mentoría | **Is there any mentorship** for new developers? |
| Un día típico | **What does a typical day look like** for this role? |
| Lo que sigue | **What are the next steps** in the hiring process? |

**El cierre:**
- Agradecer: "**Thank you for your time.** I really enjoyed our conversation."
- Interés: "**I'm excited about this opportunity.**"
- Disponibilidad: "**I'm available to start** in two weeks." / "I can start right away."
- Salario: "**Based on my research, I'm looking for** something between X and Y. **But I'm open to discussing it.**" o primero pregunta: "**What's the salary range** for this position?"
- Modalidad: "**Is this role fully remote?**" / "**What are the core hours?**" (importante si hay diferencia de horario).

## Errores típicos

- "No, I don't have questions" ✗ → "Yes, **I have a couple of questions**" ✓: no preguntar parece falta de interés.
- "How much you pay?" ✗ → "**What's the salary range** for this position?" ✓.
- "I can start immediately the next week" ✗ → "I can start **next week**" ✓: next week no lleva "the".
- "What is a typical day?" ✗ → "What does a typical day **look like**?" ✓: más natural.
- "I'm agree with the salary" ✗ → "**That works for me**" o "I agree" ✓.

## Pronunciación

- **mentorship** → méntorship y **onboarding** → ónbordin.
- **salary** → sálari: la fuerza en "sá", no "salári".
- **opportunity** → oportúniti: la fuerza en "tú".
- **negotiable** → nigóushiabol.
