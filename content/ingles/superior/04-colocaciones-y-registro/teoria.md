## En una frase

Las **colocaciones** son palabras que van juntas por costumbre: en inglés se dice *meet a deadline*, no *fulfill a deadline*; *make a decision*, no *take a decision*. El **registro** es el nivel de formalidad: no le escribes igual a un cliente que a un compañero por Slack. Dominar las dos cosas es lo que separa un inglés correcto de uno que suena nativo.

## Cómo se forma

**Colocaciones por verbo** (apréndelas en bloque, no palabra por palabra):

| Verbo | Va con | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| raise | an issue, a question, a concern | Let me raise an issue with the timeline. |
| address | an issue, feedback, a concern | We addressed all the feedback. |
| meet / exceed | a deadline, expectations | The release exceeded expectations. |
| draw | a conclusion, attention to | It's too early to draw a conclusion. |
| reach | an agreement, a decision | We reached an agreement. |
| make | a decision, progress | We made good progress this week. |
| take | ownership, into account | She took ownership of the bug. |
| pose | a risk, a challenge | This poses a security risk. |

**Raise** (plantear un problema) y **address** (ocuparse de él) van en pareja: Sarah raised an issue, and we addressed it the same day.

**Registro en tres niveles**:

| Formal (cliente, documento) | Neutro (equipo) | Informal (Slack, amigos) |
|---|---|---|
| With regard to the invoice... | About the invoice... | Quick thing about the invoice: |
| Could you kindly confirm...? | Could you confirm...? | Can you confirm? |
| to obtain, to inquire | to get, to ask | to grab, to check |
| Please submit a request. | Please send a request. | Just ping me. |
| Sincerely, | Best, | Thanks! |

**Documentación**: imperativo, frases cortas y sin opiniones: *Run the following command.* *Note that this deletes all data.*

## Errores típicos

- fulfill a deadline ✗ → **meet** a deadline ✓
- rise an issue ✗ → **raise** an issue ✓ (*rise* no lleva objeto: the price rises)
- arrive to a conclusion ✗ → **reach** o **draw** a conclusion ✓
- take a decision: se oye en Reino Unido; en EE. UU. lo natural es **make** a decision ✓
- solve a concern ✗ → **address** a concern ✓
- "Dear Tom," en Slack ✗ → "Hey Tom," ✓. "Hey guys" en un correo a un cliente ✗ → "Hi Ms. Lopez," ✓
- "Please, kindly send me..." ✗ (doble cortesía y calco) → "Could you please send me...?" ✓
- "Per my last email" no es neutro: suena a reproche ("como ya te dije"). Úsalo solo si de verdad quieres marcar eso.

## Pronunciación

- **raise** → "réiz", con *z* sonora. **rise** → "ráiz".
- **address** como verbo → "adrés", fuerza al final.
- **conclusion** → "konklúshon" y **precedent** → "président".
- **inquire** → "inkuáier": la fuerza va en "kuái".
